# MyLaw — Design System & Website Direction

## 1. Product

**MyLaw** is a legal-tech platform designed to make it easier for people to discover and connect with lawyers.

The product is currently in its **pre-launch stage**.

For this version, the website consists of ONLY:

1. Landing / About page
2. Coming Soon / Waitlist page

Do NOT build the full lawyer marketplace, search system, dashboards, booking system, or lawyer profiles yet.

The website should establish the MyLaw brand and collect early interest.

---

# 2. Core Design Direction

## Overall feeling

MyLaw should feel:

* Professional
* Trustworthy
* Clean
* Modern
* Approachable
* Calm
* Minimal
* Premium without looking expensive or flashy

The visual direction should sit between:

**modern legal technology + professional SaaS + clean editorial design**

Reference the clarity and professionalism of modern legal-tech products such as Clio and Harvey, but do not copy their layouts or branding.

## Important

This is NOT a traditional law-firm website.

Avoid making MyLaw look like:

* A law office
* A corporate law firm
* A government website
* A generic SaaS template
* An AI startup
* A luxury brand

MyLaw should feel like a **modern consumer-facing legal platform**.

---

# 3. Color Direction

The primary visual language should be **white + soft grey + deep navy**.

Keep the interface mostly light.

### Primary colors

```text
Background:       #FFFFFF
Soft Background:  #F7F8FA
Surface:          #FFFFFF
Primary Text:     #172033
Secondary Text:   #667085
Border:           #E6E8EC
Primary Accent:   #234A7A
Accent Hover:     #193A61
```

The deep blue should be used sparingly.

It represents:

* Trust
* Stability
* Professionalism
* Reliability

Do NOT use bright corporate blue everywhere.

### Optional subtle accent

A very restrained muted teal may be used for tiny highlights:

```text
#2F6F73
```

Only use this if it genuinely improves the design.

Do not introduce multiple accent colors.

---

# 4. Visual Ratio

The website should approximately feel:

```text
70% White
20% Soft Grey
8% Deep Navy
2% Accent
```

White space is a major part of the design.

Do not fill every section with cards, gradients, illustrations or decorative elements.

---

# 5. Typography

Use a clean modern sans-serif.

Preferred:

**Inter**

Alternative:

* Geist
* Manrope

Use one primary font family throughout the website.

### Typography hierarchy

```text
Hero:
56–72px desktop
40–48px mobile

H1:
48–56px

H2:
36–44px

H3:
24–28px

Body:
16–18px

Small:
14px
```

Typography should feel confident and editorial.

Avoid oversized startup-style headings that occupy half the screen.

---

# 6. Border Radius

Use restrained rounding.

```text
Small: 6px
Medium: 10px
Large: 14px
```

Avoid extremely rounded "pill everything" designs.

Pills may be used for:

* Small badges
* Status indicators
* Tags

Not for every button or card.

---

# 7. Shadows

Use extremely subtle shadows.

Prefer borders over shadows.

Example:

```text
0 1px 3px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.05)
```

Large floating shadows are prohibited.

---

# 8. Navbar

The navbar should be minimal.

### Desktop

Left:

**MyLaw**

Right:

* About
* How It Works
* For Lawyers
* Join Waitlist

The **Join Waitlist** button is the primary navbar action.

Do not create a large mega-navigation.

### Mobile

```text
MyLaw                         ☰
```

Keep the mobile navigation simple.

---

# 9. LANDING PAGE

The landing page is primarily an **About / Brand Introduction page**.

Its purpose is to answer:

1. What is MyLaw?
2. Why does it exist?
3. How does it help people?
4. Why should I trust it?
5. How can I join the launch?

---

## Section 01 — Hero

Keep the hero extremely clean.

### Small eyebrow

```text
LEGAL HELP, SIMPLIFIED
```

### Main headline

Use a strong, human statement such as:

**Finding the right lawyer shouldn't be difficult.**

Alternative:

**A simpler way to find legal help.**

### Supporting text

Explain MyLaw in 1–2 sentences.

Example:

> MyLaw is building a simpler way to discover and connect with the right legal professionals for your needs.

### CTA

Primary:

**Join the Waitlist**

Secondary:

**Learn More**

---

## Hero Visual

Do NOT use:

* Gavel
* Courtroom
* Scales of justice
* Judge
* Lawyer handshake stock photo

Instead, use one of:

### Option A — Product preview

A minimal abstract preview of what MyLaw will eventually become.

For example:

```text
----------------------------------
 Find legal help
 [ What do you need help with? ]

 Family Law
 Property
 Corporate
 Criminal
----------------------------------
```

This can be a subtle UI mockup.

### Option B — Editorial photography

A high-quality, natural photograph of a professional lawyer/client interaction.

The image should feel human rather than stereotypically "legal".

### Option C — No image

A typography-first hero is completely acceptable.

Prefer no image over a bad stock image.

---

# 10. Section 02 — The Problem

Introduce the problem MyLaw is trying to solve.

Headline:

**Legal help can feel complicated before it even begins.**

Explain briefly:

Finding a suitable lawyer often involves searching through scattered information, relying on recommendations, or simply not knowing where to start.

Keep this section short.

Do not write a huge paragraph.

---

# 11. Section 03 — The MyLaw Idea

Headline:

**We're making the first step simpler.**

Explain that MyLaw is being built to bring legal professionals and people looking for legal help together through a clearer, more accessible platform.

Use a simple 3-step visual:

```text
01
Tell us what you need

02
Discover relevant legal professionals

03
Connect with the right one
```

Keep these as simple editorial blocks rather than huge cards.

---

# 12. Section 04 — Why MyLaw

Use 3–4 principles.

### Clarity

Make finding legal help easier to understand.

### Choice

Help people discover professionals suited to their needs.

### Trust

Present useful professional information clearly and responsibly.

### Accessibility

Make the first step toward legal help easier to take.

Use minimal icons or typography.

Do not use cartoon illustrations.

---

# 13. Section 05 — Who It's For

Split the section into two sides.

### For Individuals

**Find legal help with confidence.**

A simple explanation of how MyLaw will help people discover relevant lawyers.

CTA:

**Join the Waitlist**

### For Lawyers

**Build your professional presence.**

Explain that lawyers will eventually be able to connect with people looking for legal assistance.

CTA:

**I'm a Lawyer**

This CTA can eventually lead to a separate lawyer waitlist.

For the current version, both can simply lead to the waitlist.

---

# 14. Section 06 — About MyLaw

This is the actual company/about section.

Headline:

**We're building a better starting point for legal help.**

Explain the company's mission in a human and concise manner.

Do not use generic corporate language such as:

"leveraging synergies"

"revolutionizing the legal ecosystem"

"disrupting the legal industry"

Keep the copy believable and straightforward.

---

# 15. Section 07 — Final CTA

Large clean section.

Background:

Soft grey or deep navy.

Preferred:

Soft grey background.

Headline:

**Be among the first to experience MyLaw.**

Supporting text:

> We're getting ready to launch. Join the waitlist and we'll keep you updated.

CTA:

**Join the Waitlist**

---

# 16. Footer

Minimal.

Left:

**MyLaw**

Short one-line description.

Right:

* About
* Privacy
* Terms
* Contact
* LinkedIn / Socials if available

Bottom:

```text
© 2026 MyLaw. All rights reserved.
```

Do not invent company information.

---

# 17. WAITLIST / COMING SOON PAGE

This page should be MUCH simpler than the landing page.

Think:

**Apple-like simplicity + professional legal-tech branding.**

No unnecessary sections.

---

## Layout

Large amount of whitespace.

Centered content.

```text
                     MyLaw

                  COMING SOON

          Legal help, made simpler.

       We're building a better way to
       discover and connect with legal
               professionals.

        [ Enter your email address ]

              [ Join the Waitlist ]

          No spam. Just launch updates.
```

---

# 18. Coming Soon Visual

Optional subtle visual behind or beside the content.

Possible:

* Very faint geometric line pattern
* Abstract legal/document shapes
* Minimal UI preview
* Soft gradient glow

Keep opacity extremely low.

The page should still look excellent with the visual completely removed.

---

# 19. Waitlist Form

Only ask for information that is actually needed.

### Default

```text
Email address
[ Join Waitlist ]
```

Optional:

```text
I am a:
○ Looking for legal help
○ Lawyer
```

Do not create a 5-field registration form.

The objective is **low friction**.

---

# 20. Success State

After submission:

```text
You're on the list.

Thanks for joining MyLaw.
We'll let you know when we're ready.
```

Include a subtle checkmark.

Do not use a giant celebration animation.

---

# 21. Animations

Animations should be almost invisible.

Use:

* 150–250ms transitions
* Subtle fade-ins
* Slight upward reveal
* Button hover transitions
* Navbar transitions

Avoid:

* Parallax
* 3D
* Scroll-jacking
* Huge text animations
* Excessive page transitions
* Floating objects everywhere
* Cursor effects

The website should feel **fast and confident**.

---

# 22. Responsive Design

Mobile is extremely important.

### Mobile hero

Typography should remain strong but compact.

```text
Hero H1:
40–46px

Body:
16px

Horizontal padding:
20px
```

Buttons should become full-width where appropriate.

### Mobile sections

Use:

```text
1 column
```

Do not squeeze desktop cards into tiny mobile columns.

---

# 23. UI Components

Keep the component library small.

Required:

* Navbar
* Buttons
* Text links
* Section headers
* Waitlist form
* Input
* Simple cards
* Footer
* Optional UI preview

No need for:

* Complex dashboards
* Tables
* Filters
* Search systems
* Modals
* Calendars
* Lawyer profile cards

Those belong to the future product.

---

# 24. Imagery Rules

Photography should feel:

* Natural
* Professional
* Human
* Contemporary

Avoid obvious legal stock imagery.

Never use:

* Gavel closeups
* Scales of justice
* Courtroom columns
* Judge's bench
* Lawyer pointing at documents
* Handshake stock photos
* Fake courtroom scenes

If good photography isn't available, use **UI/product visualization instead**.

---

# 25. Design Details

Use subtle visual details to make the site feel polished:

* 1px borders
* Soft grey section backgrounds
* Thin dividers
* Small eyebrow labels
* Subtle hover states
* Clean alignment
* Generous whitespace
* Strong typography

The design should rely on **spacing + typography + hierarchy**, not decoration.

---

# 26. What NOT To Do

Absolutely avoid:

❌ Black + gold luxury law-firm aesthetic

❌ Bright gradients

❌ Purple AI startup aesthetic

❌ Excessive glassmorphism

❌ Huge rounded cards everywhere

❌ Gavel / scales / courthouse imagery

❌ Generic AI illustrations

❌ Excessive animations

❌ Fake statistics

❌ Fake testimonials

❌ Fake lawyer profiles

❌ Fake reviews

❌ Claims about legal outcomes

❌ Invented company information

❌ Overly corporate copy

---

# 27. Brand Personality

If MyLaw were a person:

**Professional but approachable.**

Not:

> "We are the leading revolutionary legal ecosystem."

Instead:

> "Finding legal help shouldn't be difficult. We're building a simpler way to start."

The brand should feel like someone you would trust with an important problem.

---

# 28. Final Design Goal

The finished website should feel like:

**Modern legal platform**
+
**Clean SaaS**
+
**Professional editorial website**

with:

**white + soft grey + deep navy**

as the core visual language.

The site should look expensive because of its **simplicity, typography, spacing and execution**, not because of decorative effects.

The landing page should leave the visitor thinking:

> **"This looks trustworthy. I understand what MyLaw is. I'd be comfortable joining the waitlist."**

The coming-soon page should leave the visitor thinking:

> **"Simple. Professional. I'll join."**
